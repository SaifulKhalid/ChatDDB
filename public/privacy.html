Your privacy, honoured

ChatDDB by LabDDB — Last updated 29 July 2026

🌿 Our promise to you.

Your conversations are yours. We store them so you can pick up where you left off, not to pry. Your data is handled with care and guarded by transparent practices: every administrative action is logged and auditable, and your trust is something we work hard to earn and keep.

What we store — and why

  • Your account — just your name, email and profile picture from Google, so we know who you are and can show you your conversations.
  • Your conversations — every message and reply, saved so you can revisit, edit, or continue them anytime.
  • Your uploads — images and PDFs you attach, so the AI can see what you share (and we extract PDF text on your device so the model can read it).
  • Activity records — sign-ins, messages sent, uploads — used to keep the service healthy and detect unusual behaviour.

What we never store

  • Your IP address. We only keep a salted, truncated hash — enough to notice abuse, not enough to locate you.
  • Your browser fingerprint. User-agent strings are trimmed before storage.
  • Message content in logs. Activity records carry counts and timestamps, never what you wrote.
  • Passwords. Sign-in is handled entirely by Google — we never see a password.

Who has access

You do. Your conversations are yours alone — they are never shared between accounts, and there is no public or link-based sharing.

Service administrators may. In the rare case an administrator needs to review a conversation (e.g. to investigate abuse or debug an issue), every such access is automatically and permanently logged — who, when, and why. No part of the app can delete those audit records.

Where your data lives

  • Cloudflare — secure, global hosting for your conversations and files (D1 database and R2 storage).
  • Google (Firebase Authentication) — sign-in only.
  • AI Model Provider — receives your messages and document text needed to generate a reply, subject to their own policies.

How long we keep it

  • Conversations and files — until you choose to delete them.
  • Activity records — 90 days, then pruned automatically.
  • Incomplete uploads — cleared after 24 hours.

Your control

  • Delete any conversation from the sidebar, anytime.
  • Remove attached files from the composer before sending.
  • Sign out from the user menu to end this device’s session.
  • Request full account deletion from your administrator.

Updates

If our data practices ever change, this page will be updated and the date above refreshed.

ChatDDB is built by LabDDB — dedicated to building AI tools that respect your privacy and earn your trust.
